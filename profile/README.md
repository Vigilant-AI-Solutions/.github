# Vigilant AI Solutions

**AI-powered alerting and incident operations for modern teams.**

Vigilant AI Solutions helps organizations detect risk earlier, prioritize with confidence, and execute response actions faster.

## What we deliver

- **Alerts Command Center** for live KPI and risk visibility  
- **AI Rule Builder** for faster predictive alert creation  
- **Decision Confidence Scoring** to improve triage quality  
- **Execution-first workflows** (assignment, escalation, and resolution)  
- **Docs-first support** for faster self-serve troubleshooting
